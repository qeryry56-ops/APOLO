# RECIPE APP UI PROMPT — Plately

Generate an image of a complete modern mobile app UI design system and all screen designs for a recipe and meal-sharing app called "Plately".
Plately is a modern community-driven cooking app where people discover, save, cook, and share delicious recipes.

## Design Style
- Ultra modern 2026 startup aesthetic
- Minimalistic, clean, editorial layouts with a premium food-magazine feel
- Light mode first with warm, airy white space (`#FFFFFF` / `#FBF8F4`)
- Warm coral-orange (`#FF6B4A`) primary accent, fresh green secondary tags
- Soft rounded corners (16–20px radius), subtle drop shadows, pill-shaped buttons and filter chips

## Screens
1. **Splash Screen**: Plately logo centered, warm gradient background.
2. **Welcome / Onboarding Screen**: Full-bleed food photo, "Cook something amazing tonight", Continue with Email / Google / Apple pill buttons.
3. **Authentication Screens**: Sign In, Sign Up, rounded inputs, coral-orange primary button.
4. **Taste Preferences Onboarding**: Dietary chips (vegan, keto, gluten-free), favorite cuisines grid, skill level selector.
5. **Home / Discover Feed**: Top search bar pill, horizontal category row (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snacks, Desserts, Drinks), "Recipe of the day" hero card, vertical feed of large rounded recipe cards with metadata pills ("25 min · 4 servings · Easy").
6. **Search & Filters Screen**: Modal sheet with recent/trending searches, filter chips, "what's in my fridge" ingredient filter.
7. **Recipe Detail Screen**: Food image carousel, creator avatar, star rating, metadata pills, segmented tabs "Ingredients / Steps", sticky "Start cooking" bar.
8. **Cooking Mode Screen**: Full-screen step-by-step view, progress bar, built-in timer chips.
9. **Shopping List Screen**: Ingredients grouped by category (Produce, Dairy, Pantry), checkable rounded list items.
10. **Create / Share Recipe Screen**: Multi-step form, photo upload grid, ingredients & steps builders, "Publish recipe" button.
11. **Saved / Collections Screen**: Grid of saved recipes organized into collections.
12. **Profile Screen**: Circular avatar, bio, follower counts, "My recipes" & "Saved" tabs, settings rows.
