# AI TRIP PLANNER APP UI PROMPT — Roamly

Generate an image of a complete modern mobile app UI design system and all screen designs for an AI-powered travel planning app called "Roamly".
Roamly is a modern AI travel companion that helps users discover destinations, build personalized itineraries, organize trips, track travel plans, and share travel experiences.

The app helps users:
- Discover destinations around the world
- Generate AI-powered travel itineraries
- Organize flights, hotels, and activities
- Save places to visit
- Create day-by-day travel schedules
- Explore restaurants, attractions, and hidden gems
- Collaborate with friends on trips
- Share travel memories and photos
- Track travel budgets
- Receive personalized recommendations

## Design Style
- Ultra modern 2026 startup aesthetic
- Minimalistic, clean, editorial layouts
- Premium travel-magazine feel
- Light mode first with airy white space
- Large immersive destination photography as the hero of every screen
- Soft rounded corners (16–20px radius)
- Subtle depth, premium shadows, floating cards
- Pill-shaped buttons and filter chips
- Smooth spacing and polished interactions
- Aspirational, adventurous, inspiring vibe
- NOT cluttered, NOT corporate, NOT generic travel agency style
- Apple-level clean UI mixed with luxury travel branding

## Color Palette
- Crisp white and soft off-white backgrounds (`#FFFFFF` / `#FAFAF8`)
- Deep ocean blue primary accent (`#3A86FF`)
- Sky blue gradients and sunset orange highlights
- Deep charcoal text (`#1F1F1F`)
- Soft gray secondary text (`#8A8A8A`)
- Subtle teal and emerald accents for recommendations
- Premium glassmorphism effects on selected components

## Typography
- Clean modern sans-serif (SF Pro / Inter style)
- Large editorial destination headlines
- Strong hierarchy: bold headings, medium subheads
- Comfortable readable body text
- Prominent travel metadata pills ("4 Days", "Budget", "Family Friendly", "Adventure")

## Screens
1. **Splash Screen**: Roamly logo centered, beautiful destination image blurred in background, minimal branding, premium travel startup feel.
2. **Welcome / Onboarding Screen**: Full-screen breathtaking travel photography, soft gradient overlay, Headline: "Your next adventure starts here", Subheadline: "Let AI build the perfect trip for you", Continue with Email / Google / Apple pill-shaped buttons.
3. **Authentication Screens**: Sign In, Sign Up, Google Login, Apple Login, clean rounded inputs, ocean-blue primary CTA.
4. **Travel Preferences Onboarding**: Trip style selection chips (Adventure, Luxury, Budget, Nature, Food, Culture, Beach, Solo, Family), favorite destinations selector, budget range slider, travel pace selector, interests selection grid, top progress indicator.
5. **Home / Explore Screen**: Large search bar ("Where do you want to go?"), AI prompt input, horizontal destination categories (Beach, City, Mountains, Road Trips, Luxury, Food), featured destination hero card, personalized recommendations, trending destinations carousel, large destination cards with image, country, rating, estimated budget, trip duration, save button.
6. **AI Trip Planner Screen**: AI chat-style interface + trip generation form (Destination, Travel dates, Budget selector, Travel companions, Interests chips), Large CTA: "Generate My Trip", generated itinerary preview cards.
7. **Trip Details Screen**: Large immersive destination image carousel, overview, weather summary, budget estimate, interactive map preview, trip highlights, recommended restaurants, top attractions, Save & Share buttons.
8. **Itinerary Screen**: Timeline layout, day-by-day schedule (Morning / Afternoon / Evening), activity cards, maps integration preview, transport suggestions, editable itinerary blocks, floating add activity button.
9. **Explore Places Screen**: Search places (Restaurants, cafes, attractions, hidden gems, shopping, nightlife), interactive filter chips, large destination imagery, save-to-trip action.
10. **Budget Tracker Screen**: Trip spending summary, budget progress bars, expense categories (accommodation, food, transportation, activities, shopping), financial cards.
11. **Travel Memories Screen**: Photo gallery grid, travel journal entries, timeline layout, location tags, Share memories CTA.
12. **Saved Trips Screen**: Grid of saved trips, organized collections (Dream Trips, Upcoming, Completed, Favorites), warm empty state.
13. **Profile Screen**: Large circular avatar, travel stats (countries visited, trips completed, saved destinations), follower/following counts, Tabs (Trips, Saved, Memories), settings rows, red log out button.

## Direction
Design the app like a real App Store travel startup product. Make every screen feel connected through one cohesive design system. Generate high-quality mobile app mockups in iPhone style with realistic spacing, premium destination photography, modern travel UX, and production-ready startup design quality.
